Add your Splitwise API key

The key lets your connector use your Splitwise account. It is checked with Splitwise, then kept in your project's Secret Manager. Your AI apps never see it.

  1. Open your Splitwise apps page and choose Register your application. Open Splitwise apps
  2. Give it any name, such as My AI connector, and any homepage URL, such as this page's address. Then save it.
  3. On the app's page, choose Create API key, and copy the key.
